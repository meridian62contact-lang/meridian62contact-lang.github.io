How a desk works

Same companies, all term.

The equities belong to the desk, not to individual analysts, and they stay fixed for the term so that coverage builds week on week. Each publishing week the desk picks the one company with the most meaningful development — results, a contract, a competitor's numbers, a regulation, a commodity move — and writes 400-600 words that make one point.

Desks publish on a three-week rota, so every Monday brings three or four fresh notes and every desk writes four notes a term. See the full schedule.
