What we are

A student newsroom that follows named companies, week after week — and explains them in plain English.

Every contributor owns a short list of companies inside one sector and reads what those companies actually publish: results, filings, capital-markets days, press releases. Each week a few desks write one short note that makes one clear point. Different degrees, different countries, different languages — the same market, read from every angle.

What we do

Three things, done properly.

01

Weekly sector notes

250–400 words on one company a desk covers. What happened, what people are missing, why it matters, and what we are watching next. Every number sourced.

 02

Quarterly market recap

Four times a year, a long read that turns twelve weeks of desk coverage into the handful of things that actually connected the market.

 03

Decoded

Every specialist term we use, defined in one plain sentence. A glossary that grows with every note, so nobody needs a finance degree to read one.
