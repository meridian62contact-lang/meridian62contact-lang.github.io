## No investment advice

Meridian62 publishes educational commentary written by university students. Our notes explain what is happening at named companies and why it matters. They do not constitute investment advice, financial advice, a recommendation, an offer or a solicitation to buy, sell or hold any security or financial instrument, and they are not tailored to any reader's circumstances. Nothing on this site should be relied on as the basis for any financial decision. If you need advice, speak to a regulated professional.

## Accuracy and sources

Contributors are asked to work from companies' own published materials — results announcements, filings, presentations and press releases — and to reference the source of every figure. Despite this, Meridian62 makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of anything published. Information may become out of date, and errors are possible. Where we find one, we correct it and say so.

## Opinions

Views expressed in a note are those of the contributors named in its byline at the time of writing. They are not the views of any university, employer or other organisation with which a contributor is associated, and they may change without notice.

## Positions and independence

Contributors are asked to declare to the editors any personal holding in a company they write about, and Meridian62 does not accept payment or any other consideration in exchange for coverage. No contributor should trade on the basis of a note before it is published.

## Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Meridian62 and its contributors, editors and project leads accept no liability for any loss or damage of any kind, direct or indirect, arising from the use of, or reliance on, anything published on this site or on any linked site.

## Third-party links and content

Links to external sources are provided for verification and convenience. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content. Company names, tickers and trademarks belong to their owners.
