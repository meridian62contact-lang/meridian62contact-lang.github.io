Built from the notes

The recap starts from what the desks published, not from a blank page. Every theme it names should be traceable to specific weekly notes.

Around 2,000 words

Long enough to connect eleven sectors, short enough to be read in one sitting. Structure: what happened, the three to four threads that ran through it, what to watch next quarter.

Still plain English

The same rule as every note: someone outside finance should be able to read it start to finish. Every specialist term goes to Decoded.
